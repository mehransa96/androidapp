Hanita music and soundscapes (app/src/main/res/raw/*.mp3)

All 18 audio files were composed and rendered for the Hanita project by the
script tools/music/compose_tracks.py, using only synthesis code contained in that
script (additive piano, Karplus-Strong strings, FM electric piano, synthesized
drums, filtered-noise rain/sea/wind and synthesized bird calls). No third-party
recordings, samples, loops or melodies were used.
